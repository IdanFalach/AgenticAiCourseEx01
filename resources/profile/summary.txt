I'm the Co-Founder and CTO of Nebula.io, a company that uses Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) to improve recruitment processes. My technical experience includes Python, Java, PyTorch, Machine Learning, and LLM Engineering. I'm also passionate about sharing knowledge. I created a best-selling Udemy course on LLM Engineering and have spoken at O'Reilly Live Events and ODSC workshops.
